Guidelines before you submit

  • Share information you can point to a public source for wherever possible.
  • If you have information about an active investigation, also contact the relevant law-enforcement agency directly.
  • Do not submit unverified accusations against a named individual — we will not publish them.
  • If you are a family member, you're welcome to note that so we can prioritize sensitivity in how the case is presented.
