OPTIONAL PORTRAITS FOR SHARE CARDS
Drop a real photo here named <case-id>.jpg (or .png/.webp), e.g. roger-fortson.jpg,
and add a credit line for it in credits.json:
  {"roger-fortson": "Photo: family of Roger Fortson, used with permission"}
Only use photos the family approved or that are properly licensed/public domain.
A photo with no credit line is ignored. Never use AI-generated faces.
Then run: python3 generate_social_cards.py <case-id>
