CASE SERIES

The Freeway Phantom

Six young Black women and girls were killed in Washington, D.C. between April 1971 and September 1972. Press coverage at the time linked the deaths and gave the unidentified offender the name “Freeway Phantom.” The case remains unsolved. No one has ever been charged.

Disclaimer: This page summarizes publicly reported information for research and awareness. It does not accuse any individual of a crime and does not claim new evidence.
