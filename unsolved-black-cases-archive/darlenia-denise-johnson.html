CASE FILE #002

Darlenia Denise Johnson

Victim

Darlenia Denise Johnson

DJ
No verified photo on file

1971 · Age 16 · Washington, DC

Last Known Information

Darlenia Denise Johnson, 16, of the Congress Heights neighborhood, was abducted on July 8, 1971 while on her way to her summer job at the Oxon Hill Recreation Center. A witness reported seeing her in an old black car shortly afterward. Her body was found eleven days later, only about 15 feet from where Carol Spinks had been found.
