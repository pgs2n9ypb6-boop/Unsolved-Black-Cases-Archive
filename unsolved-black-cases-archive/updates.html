Corrections & Updates

UNSOLVED BLACK CASES ARCHIVE corrects case files when new sourcing, official records, or documented errors come to light. This page is a dated, public record of substantive corrections and additions — not routine copyediting, but changes that affect what a reader would understand about a case: a corrected date, a status update, a name added or removed, a source verified. Nothing is corrected quietly.

See How We Research for our sourcing standards, or Submit a Tip to flag something that needs review.
